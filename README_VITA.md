# Vita

Vita is the next-generation adaptive physics learning companion in this repository.

**Core spine:** physics. Mathematics is a targeted dependency layer; engineering is where understanding becomes design, measurement and building.

## Physics journey

1. **Mechanics** — motion, forces, energy, momentum, rotation, gravitation
2. **Thermal Physics** — temperature, heat, thermodynamics, statistical ideas
3. **Waves & Optics** — oscillations, waves, sound, light, interference
4. **Electricity & Magnetism** — fields, circuits, electromagnetism, Maxwell-level thinking
5. **Modern Physics** — relativity, quantum physics, atomic/nuclear physics

Modern Physics is a branch point: learners may specialize in **one** of relativity, quantum, or atomic/nuclear physics instead of being forced through all three.

## Placement and progression

A learner can enter before formal physics, during school physics, or while preparing for WAEC. Placement is based on demonstrated ability, not age or school label. Weak prerequisites are repaired only when they block the next physical idea.

The learner model is durable. Conversations can disappear or change models/devices without resetting demonstrated mastery.

## TypeScript migration

The active architecture is being migrated to TypeScript/Node. SQLite is used as a real persistence layer; tests use real in-memory SQLite databases rather than mocks. Python is not part of the target runtime.
