# Adaptive learning policy

Uden does not assume a learner's school stage from the physics topic they request.

A learner may be:

- a junior student who has never taken physics;
- already studying secondary-school physics;
- preparing for WAEC or another examination; or
- working beyond the normal school curriculum.

Placement comes from demonstrated evidence. Mathematics is treated as a dependency of physics wherever the concept requires it. When a prerequisite is weak, the engine routes the learner to the smallest actionable prerequisite instead of restarting the entire curriculum.

The learner record is durable. Sessions, mastery evidence, and current position belong to the learner model rather than the transient chat transcript.
